FAQ

Questions you might have.

How much does NOVUS cost?

We’re currently running a fixed-price 60-day Founding Agency Pilot with a small group of agencies. Ongoing pricing is agreed after the pilot, based on the commercial value NOVUS has actually created for your agency — rather than setting an arbitrary monthly fee before we have the evidence.

What happens after the 60-day pilot?

We review the commercial impact NOVUS has created — opportunities progressed, valuations generated, instructions won and attributable fee value. That gives us a real basis for agreeing an ongoing price that makes sense relative to the value NOVUS is creating.

Does NOVUS replace our CRM or existing systems?

No. NOVUS works around your existing systems rather than replacing them. It uses the information already flowing through the agency to understand what matters, where attention should go and what should happen next.

Does NOVUS automatically contact every lead?

No. Not every enquiry deserves the same action. NOVUS can prioritise, recommend, wait, route something to your team or handle an appropriate repetitive action itself depending on the context.

How much work does NOVUS create for our team?

The goal is the opposite. NOVUS should reduce the amount of manual interpretation and repetitive progression your team has to do, while directing their attention towards the opportunities where human involvement creates the most value.

How do we measure the commercial impact?

We agree what success looks like before the pilot starts, then track commercial progression through opportunities, conversations, valuations, instructions and ultimately fee income.
